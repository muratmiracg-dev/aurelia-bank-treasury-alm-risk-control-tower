# Data provenance and classification

| Layer | Content | Classification | Reproducibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| TCMB snapshot | Policy rate and USD/EUR buying rates | Official public observation | Committed CSV with source URL and date |
| BDDK snapshot | June 2026 sector assets, loans, securities, deposits, equity, profit, CAR and NPL ratio | Official public sector benchmark | Committed CSV with source URL and period |
| Aurelia positions | Loans, securities, deposits, funding and capital | Deterministic synthetic bank data | Regenerated from seed and governed templates |
| Zero curves | 19 points for TRY, USD and EUR | Synthetic calibration | Stored in YAML and generated CSV |
| Risk outputs | EVE, NII, gaps, liquidity, FX, hedges and controls | Derived analytical evidence | Regenerated by one pipeline |

## Important boundary

Official sector data calibrates context and supports benchmarking. It is never blended into
the fictional bank as if it were Aurelia Bank's confidential ledger. Source, date, unit and
classification remain visible in every external snapshot.
